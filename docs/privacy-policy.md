**Juicy Heist privacy policy**

Last updated: [TO FILL: date it goes live]

This policy explains what information Juicy Heist ("the game") collects, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by **[TO FILL: developer name]**.

## What the game does not collect

The game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any account. You do not need to sign in to play.

## What stays on your device

Your progress (levels beaten, clues found, cash and items) is saved on your device so you can carry on where you left off. It is not sent to us. Deleting the game deletes it.

## Ads

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to limit fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address and the general area it points to, and how you interact with ads.

- **If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland,** the game asks for your consent before personalized ads, and you can change your choice later from the game's settings.
- **On iPhone and iPad,** the game asks for permission before using the advertising ID to track you across other companies' apps. If you say no, you still see ads, just not personalized ones.

Google's use of this information is covered by its own policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Analytics

The game uses GameAnalytics to understand how the game is played, so it can be made better: which levels players win or lose, how long sessions last, and which device and operating system the game runs on. GameAnalytics links this to a random identifier, not to your name. GameAnalytics' policy: https://gameanalytics.com/privacy/

## Purchases

Purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made so the game can deliver it. We never see your card or payment details.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Your choices

- You can turn off personalized ads in your device settings (Android: Settings, Google, Ads; iPhone: Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking).
- You can delete all game data by deleting the game.
- You can ask us what we hold about you, or to delete it, at the address below.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
